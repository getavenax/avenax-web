## F1 — Emotion is decided first; plot is engineered backwards from it
**Evidence:** Pixar writers reportedly fix the ending emotion before writing dialogue, then build the story to earn it; the studio's core move is "find what the protagonist wants most, then throw the exact opposite at them" ([Pixar storytelling analyses](https://nofilmschool.com/pixar-story-structure), [emotional storytelling breakdown](https://www.purposestudios.in/post/understanding-pixar-s-emotional-storytelling)). **Confidence: Medium** (practitioner accounts, consistent across sources).
**Why it works:** memory is emotion-indexed — people forget plots and remember how the ending felt. Designing the feeling first guarantees the only part that survives ten years is intentional.
**Why it fails:** emotion without earned struggle reads as sentimentality; audiences detect unearned endings.
**AVENAX fit:** define the *ending feeling* of every surface first. Trace's analysis ending feeling: "I understand this chart, and I'm calmer than before I asked." The landing page's: "these people are serious." **Ethics:** clean — designing for a feeling is what all design is; faking the evidence for it is the violation. **Action:** adopt "ending emotion first" as a design-review question.

## F2 — The spine of every durable story: want vs need under escalating opposition
**Evidence:** the Pixar story spine ("Once upon a time… every day… until one day… because of that… until finally…") and Rule 19-class principles (coincidence gets characters *into* trouble, never out) recur across their catalogue ([22 Rules](https://www.toaststudio.com/en/articles/pixars-22-rules-of-storytelling/)). **Confidence: Medium.**
**Why it works:** the spine is a causality chain — each beat is a *consequence*, and human memory stores causal chains far better than event lists (the same statistical-learning machinery as `CHILDREN_PSYCHOLOGY.md` F1).
**Why it fails:** structure without specificity produces competent, forgettable stories — the template is a floor, not a ceiling.
**AVENAX fit:** build-in-public content has a native spine (goal → obstacle → consequence → lesson); every changelog and video can be a causal chain instead of a feature list. **Action:** doctrine for future media.

## F3 — Memorable characters = one silhouette, one contradiction, absolute consistency
**Evidence:** character-design practice across studios converges on: readable silhouette, a defining internal contradiction (brave AND afraid), and never acting out of character; Bluey adds the research-backed layer — grounded, everyday emotional realism outperforms spectacle for attachment ([Psychology Today on Bluey](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/positively-media/202503/7-reasons-why-bluey-is-a-masterclass-in-parenting), [150-episode resilience study](https://theconversation.com/researchers-watched-150-episodes-of-bluey-they-found-it-can-teach-kids-about-resilience-for-real-life-262202)). **Confidence: Medium; the Bluey layer Medium-High.**
**Why it works:** silhouette = instant recognition (perceptual); contradiction = inexhaustible interest (you can't finish understanding them); consistency = trust (`CHILDREN_PSYCHOLOGY.md` F3).
**AVENAX fit:** AVENAX's "character" is the brand itself: silhouette = the Baseline + monochrome; contradiction = *an AI company that keeps telling you what it won't do*; consistency = the constitution. The Media Factory (dormant) inherits this file when it wakes. **Ethics:** clean. **Action:** none now — recorded for Media Factory's design documents.

## F4 — Calm pacing outperforms stimulation for attachment (the Bluey lesson)
**Evidence:** deliberate slow pacing, self-contained everyday stories, and quiet soundscapes correlate with better attention, emotional regulation, and *parental co-viewing* — the double audience that made Bluey a global brand ([low-stimulation analyses](https://www.homeaglow.com/hub/content/low-stimulation-shows-for-toddlers); contrast evidence in `CHILDREN_PSYCHOLOGY.md` F1). **Confidence: Medium-High.**
**Why it works:** stimulation captures attention; calm *earns* it. The overstimulated viewer is present but not attached; the calm viewer builds a relationship. Bluey is watched by parents voluntarily — the strongest possible trust signal in children's media.
**Why it fails:** calm without substance is boredom; the pacing only works because every quiet minute carries emotional information.
**AVENAX fit:** this is the constitution's bet, independently validated from an unexpected domain — the calm, architectural surface is the Bluey strategy applied to fintech-adjacent AI, in a category that behaves like Cocomelon (neon, tickers, urgency). **Ethics:** clean. **Action:** none — reinforces ratified direction; cite in future design reviews when "add more energy" temptations appear.
