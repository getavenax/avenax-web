## F1 — Emotional attachment to creators is parasocial bonding, and it runs on three inputs
**Evidence:** the parasocial literature (from Horton & Wohl's original framing through modern YouTube studies) consistently identifies: **self-disclosure** (the most reliable predictor of bond formation), **direct address** (face-to-camera mimics conversation), and **repeated exposure** (algorithmic re-encounter mimics friendship's familiarity process). Followers with strong parasocial bonds trust creator recommendations "as they would the recommendations of a close personal friend" ([parasocial formation study](https://digitalcommons.spu.edu/context/honorsprojects/article/1229/viewcontent/_I_m_not_your_friend_Content_type_influence_on_parasocial_relationship__formation_on_YouTube.pdf); [Scientific Reports on parasocial effects](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-17487-3); [overview analyses](https://netpsychology.org/parasocial-relationships-why-we-feel-close-to-influencers-and-streamers/)). **Confidence: High** for the mechanism.
**Why it works:** the brain's relationship machinery doesn't require reciprocity — consistent one-directional intimacy is processed as friendship. It's the adult form of the child's character attachment (`CHILDREN_PSYCHOLOGY.md` F3).
**Why it fails / the dark side:** parasocial trust is transferable to *anything the creator sells* — which is exactly how trading-guru scams work (deepfake endorsements are literally in our scam taxonomy, `MARKET_RESEARCH.md`). Attachment built on simulated intimacy collapses on the first perceived betrayal, taking the business with it.
**AVENAX fit:** founder-led media will generate parasocial trust whether we intend it or not — the design choice is what the trust is *anchored to*. Anchor it to **methodology and honesty** (watch me build, watch me reason), never to persona worship or lifestyle.
**Ethical concerns:** this is the highest-risk finding in the system. Rules: never monetize the bond directly (no "trust me, buy this"); always route trust through verifiable work; the audience should trust *AVENAX's standards*, with the founder as evidence — not the reverse.
**Recommended action:** write these three rules into any future media playbook before the first video ships. **Priority: High (when media activates).**

## F2 — Build-in-public converts transparency into pre-launch distribution
**Evidence:** founder-journey content ("hit $5K MRR" *and* "churn spiked 20%") is consistently identified as the trust engine of indie-SaaS audiences; the single highest-converting asset is the unpolished "here's what I just built" demo ([build-in-public playbook](https://www.vibrantsnap.com/blog/build-in-public-complete-guide-founders); [founder-channel analyses](https://jonathanrintala.com/blog/build-in-public-saas-top-founders-to-follow/)). **Confidence: Medium** (industry-published, highly convergent).
**Why it works:** transparency is costly signaling (`PRODUCT_PSYCHOLOGY.md` F5) plus narrative — the audience gets a serialized story with real stakes (the Pixar spine, `ANIMATION.md` F2, running in real time), and each honest setback *raises* credibility.
**Why it fails:** performative transparency (curated "fails," revenue theater) — the audience that build-in-public attracts is precisely the audience best equipped to detect it. Also fails when the building stops feeding the content instead of the reverse (One Product Rule inversion).
**AVENAX fit:** it is already our mandated minimal mode (ADR-008: document the build journey only). The waitlist is the natural call-to-action of every build log.
**Ethics:** clean if numbers are real and consistent. **Action:** none new — this file becomes the doctrine source when the founder chooses to publish build logs.

## F3 — Durable creator businesses sell education and tools, not attention
**Evidence:** across the trading-education segment specifically, the survivors are consistency-and-depth educators (e.g., two deep videos/month) while signal-sellers churn through audience trust and burn out ([trading YouTubers review](https://www.fortraders.com/blog/top-5-best-trading-youtubers-in-2025-curated-by-expert); scam-collapse evidence in `MARKET_RESEARCH.md` F3). Ad-revenue-only creators are structurally fragile; education → product is the durable ladder. **Confidence: Medium.**
**Why it works:** educational content compounds (evergreen, searchable, referenced) while attention content depreciates in days; a product gives the compounding audience somewhere to land.
**AVENAX fit:** media as growth engine for Trace (ADR-008's exact design) — the content teaches structural chart reading; the product is the tool the education makes you want.
**Ethics:** the education must be genuinely valuable stand-alone — content that only functions as a funnel fails the transparency test. **Action:** none until media activates.
