# MEDIA_INTELLIGENCE — Permanent Research System

> Founded 2026-07-09 by founder directive. Living knowledge base — never constitutional documentation.
> **Mission:** reverse-engineer human attention and attachment. Extract principles. Never copy creators.
> **Prime rule:** never optimize for views or engagement alone. Optimize for trust, brand value, long-term loyalty, and educational value. People must return because they *want* to — not because they were manipulated. This knowledge base exists to help AVENAX become a company people genuinely love for the next 20 years.

## Finding format (mandatory)

Every finding records: **Evidence · Sources · Confidence (High/Medium/Low) · Why it works · Why it fails · AVENAX fit · Ethical concerns · Recommended action.**

## Method & update rules

- Sources: platform research, published psychology/communication studies, creator interviews, industry analyses. Peer-reviewed > industry data > creator anecdote — always labeled.
- Confidence: High = peer-reviewed or 3+ independent sources; Medium = 2 sources or industry-published; Low = single source/inference.
- Each document carries Research Date, Version, and Next Review Date (90-day default).
- Findings that fail re-verification are struck through with a dated note, never silently deleted.
- Nothing in this system authorizes engineering or content production by itself — actions route through the Founder Decision Filter and the roadmap.

## The ethical line (binding on every application)

A principle from this research may be applied only if it passes all three:
1. **Would the user endorse it if we explained it to their face?** (transparency test)
2. **Does it leave the user more capable, calmer, or better informed?** (white-hat test — competence, not compulsion)
3. **Would we be proud of it in ten years?** (the Founder Rule)

Variable-reward slot mechanics, fake scarcity, guilt loops, and attention hijacking fail these tests and are permanently out — regardless of how well they "work."
