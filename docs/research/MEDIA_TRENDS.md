## T1 — Clickbait fatigue: authenticity premium is rising
**Observed:** simplicity outperforming over-designed thumbnails; audiences "punishing exaggerated clickbait"; platform metrics (watch-time-per-impression) structurally aligned against empty clicks ([thumbnail psychology 2026](https://turborank.co/youtube-thumbnail-psychology-what-gets-clicks-in-2026/); [title data study](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/how-to-write-a-youtube-title-that-gets-clicked-research-across-11-niches)). **Confidence: Medium.**
**Read-through for AVENAX:** the calm-precision brand arrives as the market turns toward it — timing tailwind, not just taste. **Action:** none; monitor.

## T2 — AI-generated content flood → provenance becomes the scarce asset
**Observed:** AI slop is commoditizing generic content across every platform; deepfake guru endorsements already appear in the trading-scam taxonomy (`MARKET_RESEARCH.md`). The scarce goods become: verifiable authorship, consistent voice, shown work. **Confidence: Medium (directional).**
**Read-through:** build-in-public with a visible human founder is provenance by construction; AVENAX's "show the reasoning" product principle is the same defense at product level. **Action:** none now; when media activates, prefer formats that are hard to fake (live building, real screens, continuity across episodes).

## T3 — Low-stimulation media is crossing from parenting discourse to adult preference
**Observed:** the Bluey-vs-Cocomelon conversation (see `CHILDREN_PSYCHOLOGY.md` F1) is mirrored in adult media by the growth of long-form, slow, high-trust formats (long podcasts, deep explainers) alongside — not replaced by — shortform. Educational how-to holds the retention crown (42.1%). **Confidence: Medium-Low as a trend claim.**
**Read-through:** depth is a differentiator precisely because the feed punishes it short-term and rewards it long-term. **Action:** monitor; informs format choice at media activation.

## T4 — Trading/finance audiences are migrating toward verification culture
**Observed:** post-scam-wave, the surviving finance communities increasingly demand shown methodology, live track records, and third-party verification — while verification systems themselves get gamed (Myfxbook, `MARKET_RESEARCH.md` F3). **Confidence: Medium.**
**Read-through:** the demand for "logic visibility" will keep rising; Insight's reasoning-first output rides the trend. **Action:** none; re-verify at next cycle.
