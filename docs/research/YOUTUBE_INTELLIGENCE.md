## F1 — People click on *specific curiosity*, not excitement
**Evidence:** finance/business titles with concrete figures, percentages, or timeframes consistently outperform generic ones; `[year]` markers lift CTR in education/tech niches ([11-niche title study](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/how-to-write-a-youtube-title-that-gets-clicked-research-across-11-niches)). Simple thumbnails now outperform over-designed ones; audiences punish exaggerated clickbait ([thumbnail psychology 2026](https://turborank.co/youtube-thumbnail-psychology-what-gets-clicks-in-2026/)). **Confidence: Medium.**
**Why it works:** a specific number creates an *information gap with a measurable payoff* — the viewer can price the click. High-intent audiences (finance, education) are gap-closers, not thrill-seekers.
**Why it fails:** specificity that overpromises converts one click into permanent distrust; precision is a promise, and finance audiences audit promises.
**AVENAX fit:** perfect — "specific beats clever" is already brand voice law. **Ethics:** clean when the number is real; income-claim numbers are the poisoned version (scam-adjacent, permanently out). **Action:** adopt as titling doctrine when media activates.

## F2 — Retention is won in the first 30–60 seconds, by structure not spectacle
**Evidence:** 33% of viewers leave within 30s, 55%+ within a minute; a pattern interrupt in the first 5s correlates with ~23% higher retention; educational how-to leads all categories at 42.1% average retention vs vlogs at 21.5% ([2025 retention benchmark](https://www.retentionrabbit.com/blog/2025-youtube-audience-retention-benchmark-report); [hook analysis](https://www.retentionrabbit.com/blog/youtube-hook-strategy-to-keep-viewers-watching)). **Confidence: Medium.**
**Why it works:** the opening answers the only question the viewer has — "is this the video I clicked for?" Opening *inside the substance* (the chart, the problem) confirms the promise instantly; open loops ("by the end you'll see why this level matters") give the brain an unresolved pattern it wants closed — the adult version of the child's anticipation reward (`CHILDREN_PSYCHOLOGY.md` F1.4).
**Why it fails:** hooks that are pure withholding ("wait till you see this…") train the audience that your openings are empty; retention tactics decay when they stop being information.
**AVENAX fit:** native — "Here's a chart. What is this market actually doing?" is an honest hook. **Ethics:** open loops are legitimate when the loop closes with real payoff. **Action:** doctrine for future videos; no production now (ADR-008 minimal mode).

## F3 — Subscription is a trust event, not a content event
**Evidence:** watch-time-per-impression (CTR × retention) is the platform's own optimization target — it rewards *satisfied* viewers over clicked viewers ([thumbnail testing metrics](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/how-to-write-a-youtube-title-that-gets-clicked-research-across-11-niches)); consistent depth-over-frequency channels (e.g., trading educator Rayner Teo, two deep videos/month) sustain authority in finance ([trading YouTubers review](https://www.fortraders.com/blog/top-5-best-trading-youtubers-in-2025-curated-by-expert)); parasocial attachment forms through perceived authenticity and self-disclosure, and followers trust such creators like personal friends (see `CREATOR_ECONOMY.md` F1). **Confidence: Medium.**
**Why it works:** subscribing is a bet on *future* videos — the viewer subscribes when they can predict the channel's value (predictability again: the channel becomes a known episode).
**Why it fails:** channels that chase trends break their own predictability; each off-brand video spends trust.
**AVENAX fit:** one recognizable format, published on a reliable cadence, at whatever frequency quality allows. **Ethics:** clean. **Action:** format decision belongs to media activation, post-first-customer.

## F4 — Short-term viral tactics vs long-term brand assets (the separation the founder mandated)
| Short-term (works, decays, mostly rejected) | Long-term (compounds, adopted) |
|---|---|
| Shock faces, red arrows, urgency framing | Calm visual consistency — instantly recognizable, scam-proof |
| Trend-jacking uploads | Evergreen educational library that ranks for years |
| Withholding hooks | Substance-first hooks + open loops that pay off |
| Engagement bait ("comment X!") | Questions that produce genuinely useful comment data |
| Income claims / win rates | Methodology transparency (the only stance skeptics respect) |
**Confidence: Medium. Ethics:** the left column is rejected even where legal — it fails the transparency test in `README.md`. **Action:** standing doctrine.
